<objective>
Eliminate the PWA cold-launch white flash by matching `app/manifest.ts`'s colors to the app's actual dark chrome (PERCEPT-02), and remove a possible cross-region round trip to Turso by pinning the Vercel function region to Turso's actual primary region (REGION-01) — plus tighten the `/habits` e2e perf budget (D-05) to make REGION-01's improvement independently re-measurable while leaving `/`, `/study`, `/cards` at their current generous budget (D-06) since their real bottlenecks aren't touched until Phases 31/32.

Purpose: `background_color`/`theme_color` currently default to light-theme values (`#f9fafb`/`#3b82f6`), mismatching the dark chrome this app actually ships — the mismatch is what paints a white splash frame before any CSS loads. Separately, an unpinned Vercel deployment may execute in a region far from Turso's primary, adding latency to every single database round trip in the app.
Output: `app/manifest.ts`, `tests/manifest.test.ts`, `vercel.json`, `e2e/perf.spec.ts`.
</objective>
