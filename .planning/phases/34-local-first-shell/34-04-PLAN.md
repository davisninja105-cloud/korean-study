<objective>
Finish the four-route cache-first surface with Home, close the settings write-through hole,
and give the user a standing signal for "you are looking at saved data."

Purpose: Home is the PWA's launch screen, so it is the route where "paints instantly, works
offline" is most visible. Settings is the second write path the version counter cannot see
(`PUT /api/settings` does not call `bumpDataVersion()`), so like card edits it needs
write-through or it silently goes stale. And D-02's offline pill is what distinguishes
"cached but online" from "genuinely offline" — without it, LOCAL-05's success looks
indistinguishable from a bug.

Output: a cache-first `components/HomeClient.tsx` whose existing `handleSync` now also
writes through, a `patchActivitySlice` call in `components/SettingsClient.tsx`'s `save()`,
and a persistent `Offline` pill in `components/Nav.tsx`.
</objective>
